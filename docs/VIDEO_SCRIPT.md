# RedFlag demo video script

Voiceover for the submission video (3:30). Times are slide start times; about 400 words at 120 words a minute.

**Title (0:00, 5 s)**
> Good day, everyone! I'm Keshav, and this is RedFlag.

**Meet Ram (0:05, 12 s)**
> Meet Ram. He's saved fourteen hundred and fifty dollars for one IPO lot. He has a WhatsApp tip, a hype headline and three days, but not the company's real story.

**Boom and bust (0:17, 16 s)**
> And he's not alone. Last year, 267 small-company IPOs raised 1.2 billion dollars. Six months later, more than half were below their issue price, and one in four had lost over forty percent.

**Scattered signs (0:33, 12 s)**
> Why? These IPOs get lighter checks, by the exchange rather than SEBI. And the warning signs were public, but scattered across huge PDFs, regulator orders and messy names. That's the fragmented-intelligence problem we solve.

**The data (0:45, 16 s)**
> So we gathered 880 past IPOs, how each turned out, and forty-two thousand prospectus facts. Tested on unseen IPOs, our riskiest picks went bad three times as often.

**The product (1:01, 9 s)**
> Back to Ram. He uploads the prospectus and, minutes later, gets a rating, the odds and the reasons.

**Explains why (1:10, 9 s)**
> Each concern cites its source, and says whether it survived our Skeptic, an agent arguing the company's side.

**How it works (1:19, 16 s)**
> Behind the scenes, fourteen agents work as a team. First they check the bank's track record and SEBI orders, then a Planner decides how deep to dig.

**Under the hood (1:35, 24 s)**
> Our rule: code does the counting, AI reads and argues. All fourteen red flags are fixed formulas, like profit versus real cash, so they're consistent. The AI reads the complex parts and plays the Skeptic, then a tested model gives the odds.

**Zetaris (1:59, 13 s)**
> The data, IPO history, SEBI orders and prices, lives in Zetaris. Agents ask it fixed questions, see only what was known before the bid date, and every answer is traceable.

**Meterless and NVIDIA (2:12, 15 s)**
> From Meterless, Markovian reads long prospectuses window by window, World Model keeps the shared case file, and H-MEM remembers lessons. Nightshift runs every call on NVIDIA Nemotron.

**Impact (2:27, 7 s)**
> So Ram gets an analyst's homework in minutes, before his money leaves his account.

**Live demo (2:34, 56 s)**
> Let's see it live, on an IPO open this week. In seconds, it pulls the bank's twenty-one past IPOs and finds SEBI links, so it digs deeper.
>
> Now it reads the prospectus, sped up here. When one model got busy, it simply switched to another.
